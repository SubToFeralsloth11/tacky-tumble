# Tacky Tumble — store page

**Tagline:** Stick it. Roll it. Swallow the world.

**Short description (Steam, 300 chars max):**
You are a sticky ball of tape. Roll over anything smaller than you to stick it on and grow. Start out picking up crumbs on the kitchen floor, then move on to garden gnomes, then finish by swallowing a whole downtown before the clock runs out.

**Long description:**

Tacky Tumble is a short, joyful roll-up game about getting bigger.

- **Grow from 10 cm to 40 m.** You start by picking up peas, coins and sugar cubes. A few minutes later you're rolling up cats, bicycles, buses and apartment blocks.
- **Three handcrafted levels.** Kitchen Floor, Backyard and Downtown, each with its own size goal and time limit.
- **Earn up to three stars per level.** Finish faster for more stars, and use them to unlock new tape colours.
- **Keep rolling.** Once you hit the goal, carry on in timer-free free play and eat everything in sight.
- **Easy to learn.** Pick it up in seconds with keyboard, mouse or gamepad.
- **A bright, toy-like look.** Chunky shapes, plus a bouncy soundtrack where every pickup goes *pop*.

**Suggested price:** US$4.99 / A$7.50 (launch discount 20%).
