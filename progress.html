🎱 Tacky Tumble — build progress

A 3D Godot 4 "roll-up" game: you are a sticky ball of tape. Roll over anything smaller than you to stick it on and grow — from crumbs on a kitchen floor, to a backyard, to swallowing a whole downtown. Beat the size goal before the clock runs out to earn stars and unlock new tape colours.
